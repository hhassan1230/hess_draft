PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: January 31, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Museum Multiverse, a virtual reality game developed by Made in Brooklyn Games. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting the personal information of all players who interact with our game on the Meta Quest platform and other supported platforms.

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. This policy applies to Museum Multiverse and any related services provided by Made in Brooklyn Games.

By playing Museum Multiverse, you agree to the practices outlined in this Privacy Policy. You can also find more details on our main site at hessvacio.com/pages/museummultiverse.html.

2. What Data We Collect

We do not collect personal data directly from players. However, we may receive anonymized or aggregated data provided by the platform (e.g., Meta Quest, PICO, SteamVR) for gameplay analytics and performance monitoring.

We may process the following types of data:

  • Gameplay Data: Session time, game progress, in-game interactions, and achievements.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, hardware specifications, and performance metrics provided by the platform.
  • User Communication: If you contact us for support, we may collect your email address and any necessary details to assist you.

We do not collect, process, or store:

  • Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your full name, address, or phone number.
  • Biometric data, voice recordings, or camera input.
  • Financial or payment data (handled directly by the platform store).

3. How We Use Your Data

We use anonymized data strictly for the following purposes:

  • Game Improvement: Enhancing Museum Multiverse based on usage trends and feedback.
  • Bug Fixes & Support: Identifying and resolving technical issues to improve user experience.
  • Compliance & Security: Ensuring compliance with platform policies and legal requirements.

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties for marketing or advertising.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

  • We only retain data for as long as necessary to provide the game experience and fulfill legal obligations.
  • If you wish to delete your data, you can request this through the platform you purchased the game from (e.g., Meta Quest settings).
  • Any direct inquiries for data deletion can be sent to info@mibkg@gmail.com, and we will coordinate with the platform provider to process your request.

5. Your Rights

As a user, you have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal data we may have (if applicable).
  • Request deletion of your data (through platform settings or by contacting us).
  • Opt-out of marketing communications (if subscribed).

For privacy requests, email info@mibkg@gmail.com.

6. Children’s Privacy

Museum Multiverse is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If a parent or guardian believes their child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take appropriate action.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to comply with platform or legal requirements. We will notify users of any significant changes via our website museummultiverse.com and recommend checking this policy periodically.

8. Contact Information

For any privacy-related concerns, reach out to us at:
Email: info[dot]mibkg@gmail[dot]com
