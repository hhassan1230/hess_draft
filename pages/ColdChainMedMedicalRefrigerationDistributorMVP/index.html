The problem

A catalog full of cabinets isn't a buying tool.

Most distributor sites list refrigerators like office furniture — a brand, a price, a photo. But a lab director with a +2°C to +8°C requirement and an NSF mandate can't shop that way. They need to filter by the band they're obligated to hold, and confirm compliance long before they ever ask about price.

Out of band

+11°C in a vaccine fridge — a reportable loss, and a buyer who never finds the right unit.

In band

Filter to +2→+8°C, CDC-Vaccine certified, 2.2 cu.ft. — three clicks to the exact match.

How it works

Three steps from requirement to qualified lead.

STEP 01

Filter by what matters

Temperature band, compliance marks, capacity, lock and monitoring — the same axes a buyer already has on their requisition. The grid updates instantly.

STEP 02

Match the exact unit

Spec-accurate detail pages: temperature range, refrigerant, dimensions, monitoring method — modeled on real cold-chain attributes, not marketing copy.

STEP 03

Hand off a precise lead

One request sends a structured, SKU-tagged inquiry to your specialist queue. Your team knows the facility, the unit, and the question before they reply.

The signature filter

Shop by the band you have to hold.

Drag the target temperature. The catalog keeps only the units that can hold at or below it — the way a buyer with a storage obligation actually thinks. No conversion, no guesswork.
