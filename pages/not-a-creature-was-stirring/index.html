Decorate the tree before midnight. The nutcrackers only move when you look away.

Open this page in the Meta Quest Browser to play in your own room.

Judges' link: every night is open after a win.

Start on night
  • Sit down, ideally with a table in front of you. No table? A small one appears.
  • Pinch an ornament, then let go next to the tree to hang it.
  • Anything you can see freezes. Stare a nutcracker down to send it back and win back the ornament it stole.
  • Three candles are your lives. Hands only, no controllers needed.

Contains jump scares, sudden loud sounds and red flashes.

Made in Brooklyn Games · Melody: “Silent Night” (Franz Gruber, 1818) · Nutcracker and Mouse King from E.T.A. Hoffmann (1816) · Title from “A Visit from St. Nicholas” (1823)
